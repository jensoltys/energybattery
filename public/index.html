How Energy Battery works

The whole app in two minutes.

The big picture

Your phone tells you it's at 40% and you plan around it. Your body doesn't. This app makes it. A few times a day you say how charged you feel and what you were doing, and over a couple of weeks that turns into a picture of what actually charges and drains you.

Glow is the firefly on the left. Her tail fills and glows with your day, and she'll say something if you click her.

Logging a check-in

  1. Slide the energy number. 1 is fumes, 10 is fully charged. First instinct is the useful one.
  2. Pick the time of day.
  3. Tap what you were doing. As many tags as fit.
  4. Say what charged you and what drained you, if anything stands out. Both optional.
  5. Log It.

Two or three check-ins a day beats one. One tells you today was a 6. Three tell you that you were a 3 in the morning and an 8 after lunch, which is the thing you can plan around.

The battery and the streak

LowRunning down. Glow goes dim.
MiddlingGetting through it.
ChargedFull.

The level is today's average across your check-ins, not your last one. The streak counts days where you logged anything at all, so a 2 out of 10 day still keeps it alive. Those are the days most worth having in the data. The backup cell under the streak covers one missed day a week, so a single off day won't reset it.

What each tab is for

  • Check In is the form. Start here.
  • Battery is today: your level and how the day has gone.
  • Log is your history, most recent first.
  • Patterns is what the app noticed. That one's Pro.

Free and Pro

Free gives you unlimited check-ins, the battery, the streak and the last 7 days of history. Pro opens your full history and pattern insights: which activities line up with your good days and your crashes, and what time of day you're reliably at your best. Nothing is deleted on free, older check-ins are just tucked out of view.

On this copy everyone is on the free plan. Pro is part of the hosted version at energybattery.app.
